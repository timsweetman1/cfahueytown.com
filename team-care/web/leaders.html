Leader pilot

  1. Open the employee portal and try rides, childcare, Direct2Care and the uniform review.
  2. Create a practice request below. Assign yourself, mark it in progress, then resolved.
  3. Check that real request totals do not include the practice request.
  4. Try the AI resource finder once its connection is ready. Direct contacts should always be available.

Practice requests are labeled TEST, contain no employee data and send no email. Real intake stays closed until email is connected.

Connection setup

Groq needs a server-side GROQ_API_KEY, GROQ_ENABLED=true, and a free-plan account. Enable zero data retention in Groq before using employee questions. The pilot has a site-wide cap of 100 AI calls per UTC day; it falls back to topic matching on errors or quota limits.

Email needs a verified sender and Resend connection. Do not enter API keys in employee forms or request notes. Sign in with the exact approved email address; receiving mail at an address alone does not grant dashboard access.
